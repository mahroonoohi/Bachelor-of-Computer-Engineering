### 🎓 Bachelor’s Final Project 
**May – August 2024**  
**Visualization of Data Structures and Algorithm Design**  
*Supervisors: Dr. Fatemi and Dr. Shafiei*  

🏆 *Awarded 2nd Best Bachelor’s Thesis at the University of Isfahan, Faculty of Computer Engineering.*

**Highlights:**
- Created visual representations for structures such as **Array**, **Linked List**, **Stack**, **Queue**, and various **Trees**.  
- Generated **step-by-step algorithm workflows** and corresponding **LaTeX/TikZ diagrams**.  
- Published as an open-source package on [PyPI](https://pypi.org) under the name **`algorithm_visualization_library`**.
